THE SWIM

Train to swim without a wetsuit

11%
Wetsuits improved swim performance by up to 11% in studies, mostly in pools.
Swim open water often, not just the pool. In the final 8 weeks, 2 to 4 swims a week, mixing both.
Never swim open water alone.
