THE HARDEST SECTION

The long climb to the high point

BIKE · MI 31 TO 45 (KM 50.6 TO 72)
Key long rides carry the course’s climbing: about 47 ft per mile (9 m per km).
Practice race power on climbs as long as the course’s longest one. Then practice the descents off the top.
Coaches’ guidance, not studies: Kleanthous, 220 Triathlon 2022
