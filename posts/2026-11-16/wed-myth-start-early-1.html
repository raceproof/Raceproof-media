MYTH
Start everything as early as possible.
PROOF
Some adaptations fade. Time each one.
WEEKS BEFORE RACE DAY
Heat
Build 6 to 2 weeks out. About 2.5% fades for every day without heat.
Downhill
Biggest weeks 5 to 3 weeks out. Last big session 10 to 14 days before.
Gut
Start 12 to 8 weeks out. Race-level carbs by 4 to 6 weeks out.
Climbing
All block long. It lasts well.
General guidelines, not a prescription.
Windows: Raceproof adaptation windows · Heat fade: Daanen et al. 2018 · Downhill protection lasted about 6 weeks: Byrnes et al. 1985
