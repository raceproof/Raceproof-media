THE TEST

One race, one number to check

01Pick the pace you could hold for the whole race.
02Run mile 1 at it, not at the crowd’s pace.
03Afterward, compare mile 1 with your average pace.
04More than 5% faster? You went out too fast.
The 5% line comes from the marathon study (Dong & Li 2026).
