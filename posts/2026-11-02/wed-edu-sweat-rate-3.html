THE MATH

Weight lost plus what you drank, divided by hours.

1 lb lost is about 16 oz of sweat (1 kg is about 1 L)
JONATHAN'S 1.5 HOUR RUN
Weight lost 5.0 lb(2.3 kg) = 80 oz (2.3 L)
Drank on the way 25 oz(0.74 L)
Total sweat 105 oz(3.1 L)
Sweat per hour 70 oz(2.1 L)
His number, not a target. Sweat rates vary a lot from person to person.
